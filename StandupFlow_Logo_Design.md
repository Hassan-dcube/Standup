## Color Scheme Suggestions

- **Primary**: Deep Blue (#1e40af) for "StandupFlow"
- **Secondary**: Green (#10b981) for "AI-Powered"
- **Accent**: Orange (#f59e0b) for highlights
- **Background**: Light Gray (#f8fafc) or White
- **Text**: Dark Gray (#374151)

## Design Elements

- **Font**: Modern, clean sans-serif (like Inter, Roboto, or Helvetica)
- **Icons**: Use flow arrows (→), brain/gear icons, or progress bars
- **Layout**: Centered, with plenty of white space
- **Style**: Professional, tech-forward, clean and minimal

## PowerPoint/Google Slides Tips

1. Use a gradient background (blue to light blue)
2. Add subtle animation (fade in for title)
3. Include a small tagline below the main title
4. Use consistent spacing and typography
5. Consider adding a subtle pattern or texture
